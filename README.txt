OpenQuatt connects Homey Pro directly to the OpenQuatt heat pump controller on your own network. The controller is discovered automatically and all data arrives in real time over a live local connection — no polling and no cloud account needed. Monitor supply, return, outside and room temperatures, the supply target, power consumption, the active control mode and the aux relay, and switch manual cooling or the relay straight from Homey.

Automate everything with an extensive set of Flow cards for heating, cooling, faults, defrosting, the boiler, silent mode and the water flow, and feed the dew point from your own room sensors so the controller can cool safely without condensation. Two dashboard widgets: a status card with live temperatures, power, efficiency and status at a glance, and a heat pump card that shows each outdoor unit up close, with its compressor, fan, water temperatures and flow. Away from home, an optional read-only page shows the controller through your Homey's cloud connection, signed in with the controller's own login. Requires a Quatt heat pump running the OpenQuatt firmware and a Homey Pro (Early 2023 or newer). This app is a community project and is not affiliated with Quatt B.V.
